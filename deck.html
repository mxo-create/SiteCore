The Problem

ASGM doesn't suffer from a shortage of gold.
It suffers from a shortage of trust.

  • Responsible miners struggle to find responsible buyers
  • Buyers struggle to identify responsible mining sites
  • Governments struggle to understand what's happening on the ground
  • Communities struggle to raise concerns
  • Support providers struggle to identify serious operations

The information exists. The relationships don't.

What Is SiteCore?

An independent trust coordination organization for artisanal and small-scale gold mining.

SiteCore IS

  • The trust layer connecting the ecosystem
  • Field-verified, progressive trust profiles
  • A coordinator — not a certifier or marketplace

SiteCore is NOT

  • A gold marketplace or payment platform
  • A certification body or regulator
  • A blockchain project or government registry

Core Philosophy

Most systems ask: "Can this mine be trusted?"

SiteCore asks: "How can this site become more trusted?"

Trust through evidence · Improvement before exclusion · Human verification

MVP Scope

One pilot region · Gold only · 4 core user roles

In MVP

  • 6 role views with top-bar switcher
  • Progressive trust tiers & location trust
  • Field checklist assessments & GPS capture
  • Site map (offline schematic)
  • Buyer discover & service network
  • Community reports & government dashboard
  • Offline queue & one-click demo mode

Post-MVP

  • Real messaging & payment integrations
  • Live tiled maps & routing
  • Multi-commodity registry API
  • Advanced analytics & revenue features
